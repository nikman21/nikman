A recording can be valuable before it has a title, a transcript, a folder, or an internet connection.

In [Osef](/work/osef/), the original saves locally before optional private sync. Someone recording a thought should be able to finish even when the network is unavailable.

Syncing a copy and giving an agent access are separate choices. Signing in shouldn’t automatically enable both.

## The source is worth keeping

A title, transcript, or agent note can help someone find a capture again. Keeping these separate from the original lets someone correct an interpretation without losing the source.

## What I am still figuring out

How should an app explain a local original, a synced copy, and an agent’s access without putting a small infrastructure dashboard in the middle of someone’s thought?

I want those choices to be clear without making every capture feel like setup.
