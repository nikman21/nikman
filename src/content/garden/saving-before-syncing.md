A recording can be valuable before it has a title, a transcript, a folder, or an internet connection.

In [Osef](/work/osef/), the original saves locally before optional private sync. That is a product decision as much as a storage decision: the thing a person came to do should succeed without making them understand the rest of the system.

Sync is a second operation. Agent access is another choice again. Signing in should not silently collapse all three into one action.

## The source is worth keeping

An extracted title, transcript, or agent interpretation can help someone return to a capture. Each is also different from the original. Keeping those layers separate makes it possible to correct an interpretation without losing what was actually saved.

## What I am still figuring out

How should an app explain a local original, a synced copy, and an agent’s access without putting a small infrastructure dashboard in the middle of someone’s thought?

I am working through this question in [Osef](/work/osef/). The everyday interface should make the important parts much quieter.
