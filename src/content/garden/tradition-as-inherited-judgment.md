Tradition is the transmission of practices, beliefs, rituals, stories, and institutions across generations.

I am drawn to tradition because it challenges the assumption that the present automatically understands more than the past. A practice that has survived may contain judgment no single person invented: ways of worshiping, governing, learning, celebrating, mourning, and making meaning.

Survival is not proof of goodness. Traditions can preserve errors, exclusions, and power structures alongside wisdom.

I want to ask what a tradition protects and what it prevents, as well as how long it has lasted.

Receiving an inheritance requires humility toward the people who carried it and responsibility toward the people who will receive it next.
