## Sometimes I want to tune in

Fresh TV explores a familiar idea on the web: channels with a schedule, an on-screen guide, and a player that stays with you while you browse.

It gives my interests in technology, history, travel, food, and media a different home from a search box or an endless recommendation feed.

## Choosing what goes on air

The visible experience depends on quieter work: approving a video, deciding where it belongs, preparing a lineup, checking availability, and choosing when a schedule should become public.

## How it works

The project combines a web viewer, an official YouTube player, and PostgreSQL-backed programming. The admin work includes review, channel assignments, dated news editions, and schedule preparation.
