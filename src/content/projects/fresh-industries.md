## Fix Today. Build Tomorrow.

I co-founded Fresh Industries for our software, creative media, and services. I also want room to start businesses in other industries.

## The work today

[InflateMate](/work/inflatemate/) helps party-rental operators manage bookings, inventory, payments, and waivers. It has paying customers, and their feedback shapes my work on it.

Other work includes [Fresh Infra](/work/fresh-infra/) for shared application foundations, [Bara](/work/bara/) for creative editing, and [Osef](/work/osef/) for personal capture. [Fresh TV](/work/fresh-tv/) and [The Line List](/work/the-line-list/) take those interests into video programming and manufacturing workflows.

## Beyond software

Longer term, I want to work in hardware and manufacturing, too. Fresh Industries is still early.
