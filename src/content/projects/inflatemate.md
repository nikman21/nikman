## The work

InflateMate is software for inflatable and event-rental companies. It helps owners manage bookings, inventory, payments, waivers, and customer workflows without relying on scattered spreadsheets and disconnected tools.

## Why I built it

I started building InflateMate after college when I could not get a job in technology. Rejection forced me to become more creative and make something of my own.

What began as a response to a setback became a real product and the place where I keep learning how engineering, design, customer understanding, trust, and operations meet.

InflateMate is also a clear expression of the Fresh Industries philosophy: **Fix Today. Build Tomorrow.** The inflatable and event-rental industry has been underserved by modern software, so the work begins by improving the tools operators rely on today.

## The lesson

Software is never only code. It is also the promise that a system will behave when someone depends on it. Building for an overlooked industry has made reliability, clarity, and respect feel concrete.
