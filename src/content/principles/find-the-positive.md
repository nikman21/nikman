title: Find the Positive in Every Negative
summary: Every negative situation contains the possibility of something positive. A setback can reveal the wrong environment, awaken drive, force creativity, teach a lesson, or redirect me toward something I would not have found otherwise.
question: What can I learn, change, or build because this happened?
practices:
  - Name the negative honestly without allowing it to define the whole story.
  - Look for the lesson, opportunity, or change in direction hidden inside the setback.
  - Redirect frustration and disappointment into the next constructive action.
  - Remember that today's negative outcome may become the beginning of something better.
whyChosen: This is one of the main principles by which I already live. Setbacks in wrestling and after college taught me that a bad outcome does not have to have the final word.
status: accepted

I can acknowledge pain and loss while looking for something I can learn or change. Sometimes I have to create the opportunity myself.

I learned this through wrestling. Being removed from a team and changing schools felt like rejection, but it forced a new direction. I eventually won a state championship. The negative event did not become good; it became material I could use.

The same pattern returned after college when I could not get a job in technology. That rejection pushed me to create work of my own. InflateMate grew from the next constructive action I could take.

I want to keep taking responsibility for what I do next, even when the situation is hard.
