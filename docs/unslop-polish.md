# Unslop polish — November 2, 2026

Applied [Unslop by Damir Sokolovsky (@heydamir)](https://github.com/heydamir/unslop), CC BY 4.0, using its installed skill and signs, tokens, and visuals references. This is a polish pass within the user's existing personal-home direction. No new theme, generated assets, paid work, publication, or vault edits.

## Audit

- Home section headings repeat the same reflective slogan pattern rather than identifying their content.
- Small labels, navigation, dates, and categories use uppercase with wide tracking throughout the site.
- Page titles such as “Things I wanted to exist” use space without helping visitors identify their location.
- Page entrances have more padding than their short titles need.
- The footer repeats the homepage's purpose as another large statement.
- Project images zoom on hover without adding useful feedback.
- Fresh Infra's signing model and Fresh TV's fictional guide do not represent the work in a way the owner wants. Both are explicitly rejected.
- Fresh TV is missing the actual application destination supplied by the owner.

The palette, paired typefaces, distinctive landscape artwork, sourced project images, clear stage labels, and reading layout already have a direction. They do not need replacement.

## Direction

- **Product:** Nik's personal home for projects, writing, videos, and current interests.
- **Character:** Curious, practical, warm.
- **World:** A paper notebook framed by a cobalt landscape.
- **Moments:** Existing orange-tree artwork and real project screenshots, exports, and videos.
- **Signature:** Fine editorial rules with restrained orange accents.

The user requested implementation and then this polish pass, so work continued within the existing direction. No new direction or asset-generation decision was introduced.

## Passes

1. **Spacing:** Reduce shared index introductions to 32–48 px vertical padding. Bring project text closer to its introduction and remove the first Markdown heading's inherited top margin. Keep the established section rhythm and responsive order.
2. **Color:** Retain the paper/ink/cobalt/orange palette and existing focus treatment. No new tint, gradient, glow, or decorative surface.
3. **Icons:** Keep the existing wordmark, citrus mark, and simple directional arrows. No icon set or decoration added.
4. **Copy:** Use direct section headings such as What I'm building, Recent writing, and Videos. Name index pages plainly. Remove duplicate section labels, the footer slogan, and the two rejected examples. Describe the real toolkit and link Fresh TV to its supplied application address. Give the remaining artifact/example links specific labels.
5. **Type:** Change interface labels and navigation to sentence case with 0.01 em tracking. Increase shared labels to 14 px. Keep Newsreader and IBM Plex Sans; preserve the existing uppercase wordmark as a deliberate brand detail.
6. **Layout:** Keep sections in their current order and retain the text/image split, project grid, reading column, and phone stacking. Remove the redundant volume field from the header. Put footer destinations in two short columns alongside the social links.
7. **Details:** Let the project title lead; stage and technology metadata stay secondary. Preserve usable native controls and focus indicators.
8. **Visuals:** Keep real assets and existing art. Fresh Infra and Fresh TV get no replacement fictional interfaces. Fresh TV's domain could not be visually inspected because DNS lookup failed here; use the owner's exact destination.
9. **Motion:** Remove project-image zoom. Keep quiet link feedback and the existing reduced-motion treatment.

No image prompt or story video was needed for this pass. Historical QA of the removed examples is not a claim that they remain on the refreshed pages.
