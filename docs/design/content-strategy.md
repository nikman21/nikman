# Content Strategy

Date: 2026-08-01

## What the site should communicate

The site is a curated public identity, not a résumé, product funnel, or public copy of Nik's private vault.

Visitors should understand:

1. Nik builds useful software and systems.
2. InflateMate is the clearest proof of that work.
3. Fresh Industries is Nik's umbrella company and the long-term home for improving what exists today and building what should exist tomorrow.
4. Nik studies enduring subjects including faith, history, humanity, design, and technology.
5. Writing and principles are part of the same practice as building.
6. This is a beginning with real evidence, not a performance of having already arrived.

## Core narrative

The homepage moves through four ideas:

1. Identity: Making something out of nothing.
2. Practice: Build, study, write.
3. Evidence: InflateMate and Fresh Industries.
4. Horizon: the question, What am I doing today that will still matter in five years?

The conceptual story is Stone & Current:

- Stone holds principles, faith, history, and what endures.
- Current holds software, experiments, business, learning, and the present.

## Primary audience

- Builders and founders.
- Curious software developers and designers.
- Potential collaborators and customers evaluating Nik's judgment.
- Readers interested in technology, faith, history, design, and meaningful work.

## Navigation

Primary:

- Explore
- Build
- Essays
- Garden
- Current
- About

The wordmark returns home, so a separate Home label is unnecessary. Explore is the archive map and explains how every public area differs. Principles and Commonplace remain first-class destinations inside Explore and the footer without competing for primary navigation space.

Content roles:

- Essays: finished thinking.
- Garden: ideas still growing.
- Commonplace: things worth remembering.
- Principles: ideas Nik is trying to live.
- Current: what has Nik's attention now.

## Work

Purpose: prove that Nik builds real systems.

Launch feature:

InflateMate — software for inflatable and event-rental companies. It helps owners manage bookings, inventory, payments, waivers, and customer workflows.

Safe founder context:

Nik started building InflateMate after college when he could not get a job in technology. Rejection became a reason to create something of his own and a place to learn how engineering, design, customer understanding, and operations meet.

Fresh Industries:

Fresh Industries is Nik's umbrella company and is not limited to software or one industry. Its official slogan is “Fix Today. Build Tomorrow.” “Fix Today” means improving outdated, underserved, inefficient, fragmented, or poorly designed industries, systems, workflows, products, and experiences. “Build Tomorrow” means creating new systems, products, technologies, experiences, companies, and ideas that may not exist yet.

InflateMate is one current example of “Fix Today.” Future areas such as AI, automation, games, media, food, manufacturing, physical products, and experimental ventures are long-term possibilities—not claims about current operations. Present Fresh Industries as early, grounded, patient, practical, useful, design-led, systems-oriented, and future-facing.

Do not publish unverified:

- Customer counts.
- Revenue.
- Product screenshots.
- Roadmap items.
- Awards.
- Unsupported outcome claims.

## Essays

Purpose: show how Nik thinks and learns.

Launch essay:

Why I Built a Personal Encyclopedia Instead of a Productivity System.

The essay may be lightly adapted for web readability while preserving Nik's candid voice.

Essay taxonomy at launch:

- Building
- Technology
- Design
- Faith and meaning
- History and humanity

Use one archive with labels. Do not create a top-level page for each interest.

Structured note fields:

- Title.
- Slug.
- Description.
- Published date.
- Updated date.
- Category.
- Status.
- Featured flag.
- Related principles.
- Social image.

Only published notes appear in indexes, RSS, sitemap, or search.

## Principles

Purpose: present the small set of ideas Nik has consciously chosen to practice.

Launch principles:

- Build What Compounds.
- Find the Positive in Every Negative.

Principles are personal standards, not universal commandments. Each includes:

- The principle.
- A returning question.
- Practices.
- Why Nik chose it.
- Related public writing.

Never expose candidate principles or candidate counts.

## Current

Purpose: a dated snapshot, not a task list.

Launch-safe content:

- Building InflateMate under Fresh Industries.
- Learning across design, systems, history, faith, and AI.
- Practicing writing, software building, attention, and a more consistent spiritual life.
- Thinking about trust, durable institutions, ambition, humility, and obvious interfaces.

Current must display a last-updated date. Items that change quickly should be confirmed before updates are published.

## About

Purpose: connect builder, learner, faith, and long-term direction without reading like a professional bio.

Key ideas:

- Nik is a builder and founder.
- His interests look broad but return to trust, durability, meaning, and human flourishing.
- InflateMate taught him that software includes promises, operations, and relationships—not only code.
- The site records work in progress rather than a finished self.
- Family, faith, capability, and responsibility matter, but private details should not be invented or overexposed.

Do not invent location, age, education, contact details, social profiles, denomination, family details, or credentials.

## Voice

Writing should be:

- Plain.
- Reflective.
- Concrete.
- Honest about uncertainty.
- Ambitious without inflated claims.

Avoid:

- Generic founder language.
- Self-congratulatory claims.
- Empty futurism.
- Motivational slogans without personal evidence.
- Marketing superlatives.

## Content source boundaries

Public now:

- The launch essay already marked ready for publication.
- The two accepted principles.
- Safe descriptions in this strategy.

Editorial pass required:

- A Run Without Technology.
- Long-term games reflection.
- Society and government reflection.

Private until explicit approval:

- Spiritual-practice drafts.
- Principle candidates.
- Raw capture notes.
- Learning dashboards.
- Archive and system files.
- Personal journals.
- Unverified product details.

The vault is the workshop. The website is the exhibition.

## Content architecture

Use Astro Content Collections for:

- Notes.
- Projects.
- Principles.

Use typed data for:

- Site metadata.
- Navigation.
- Current snapshot.

Content should be added by creating a Markdown or MDX file with validated frontmatter. No CMS is needed for version one.

## Launch content checklist

- Homepage reads clearly without invented metrics.
- InflateMate description contains only verified stable capabilities.
- Launch essay preserves Nik's voice.
- Both accepted principles are public.
- No candidate or private content ships.
- Current is visibly dated.
- All public pages have titles and descriptions.
- Draft notes are excluded from public lists and feeds.
